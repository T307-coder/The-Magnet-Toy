v1.2.6 — Default settings overhaul, semiconductor EM exclusion, SPRK charge redistribution, ferromagnet dedup.
Default: new induction (O), induction SPRK (Z), particle gravity (L) ON. Old induction (I) OFF (not recommended).
Q key (free charge fields) default OFF. S key (potential-driven SPRK) stays OFF.
Semiconductors (PSCN/NSCN/PTCT/NTCT) excluded from all EM: no charging, polarization, induction, force.
Ferromagnet magnetization block (BMTL/BRMT/IRON/TTAN) deduplicated into magnetism_ferromagnetUpdate().
Magnetized ferromagnets keep life=100 to block old induction; induced SPRK life stays short (4).
SPRK charge redistribution: conducting SPRK instantly averages charge with receiver.
Unified Q key for non-solid charge fields, per-element eSrc gating.
