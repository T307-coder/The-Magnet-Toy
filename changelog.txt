v1.1.2 — Async fields, field extrapolation, induced SPRK fix.
Default async B/E field solver (same Exchange pattern as Newtonian gravity).
T key: temporal field extrapolation (grid-level, factor 0.5).
O key: source position prediction (particle-level, writes at predicted next-frame positions).
Induced SPRK (dB/dt) no longer contribute to Biot-Savart current field.
Induced flag propagates through all SPRK conduction paths.
Q key: toggle ELEC/PROT field production.
Full coverage: all conductors, magnetic monopoles (MGPN/ELMG/MAGN), gravity sources (NBHL/GBMB).
