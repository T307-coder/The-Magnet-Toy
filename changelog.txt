v1.2.8 — Induction SPRK now triggers on local charge *difference* (≥16) between neighbours, not absolute charge.
This allows unlimited charge accumulation as long as adjacent conductors have similar potential.
Dielectric breakdown only at steep potential gradients (strong local E-field), matching real physics.
New induction direction: both axes independently computed, allowing diagonal electron drift.
Semiconductor EM exclusion (PSCN/NSCN/PTCT/NTCT).
Default: new induction (O), induction SPRK (Z), particle gravity (L) ON. Old induction (I) OFF.
