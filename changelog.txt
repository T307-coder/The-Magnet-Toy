v1.2.2 — Plasma EM, charge heredity, universal force pass, THRM support.
Plasma (PLSM) and hot ionised gases (HYGN>2000°C, NBLE>2000°C, OXYG>3000°C, CO2>4000°C) now polarise, hold charge, and respond to E/B fields.
Molten LAVA (>1500°C) acts as ionic liquid with full EM response.
THRM (>2000°C) conducts charge and inherits it from BREC reactions.
Charge conserved across all phase transitions (melt/solidify via part_change_type).
Centralised eSrc, Coulomb force, and Lorentz force pass for ALL charged particles (non-conductors included).
Particle gravity field (L key): all Gravity>0 elements source gravity.
Electret effect now possible: insulators inherit permanent charge from LAVA solidification.
Ion thruster and tokamak-like configurations now feasible with charged plasma + E/B fields.
