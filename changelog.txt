Electromagnetism mod initial release.
Magnetic & electric fields with Lorentz force, dielectrophoresis, induction.
B-field via Biot-Savart + FFT Poisson solver. Ferromagnetic elements (IRON, BMTL, BRMT, BREC, TTAN, TESC, GOLD, TUNG, PTNM, LITH).
Electric field with Coulomb interaction. Circuit-compatible conductors (METL, PSCN, NSCN, INST, SWCH, INWR).
ELEC/PROT charged particles. ELMG/MGPN magnetic sources. SPRK induction via dB/dt.
UBFM/EBFM uniform B/E field elements.
Q key to toggle ELEC/PROT field production. Lua bindings for magnetism/electricity control.
