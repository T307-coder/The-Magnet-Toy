v2.0.16 — Dual magnetization model: induced tmp3 + permanent ctype.
Induced (tmp3): MAGN/ELMG/coil contact, ctype pump, DEUT diffusion, eddy decay.
Permanent (ctype): Curie quench only, locked in lattice, no diffusion or self-charge.
Eddy current (V key): surface decay at any missing neighbour, uniform rate, heats unmagnetized.
Curie quench (W key): cooling through 773K freezes ctype, sentinel resets for next cycle.
dB/dt threshold 0.001→0.05 filters magnetization noise. New induction skips magnetized iron.
